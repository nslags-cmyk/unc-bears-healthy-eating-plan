Build a personalized food-group plan for your dietary analysis project.

Enter your age, sex, height, weight, and typical physical activity level to estimate a daily calorie target and MyPlate-style food-group goals. Then print or save your results as a PDF for Canvas submission.

Your information

How to use it

Student directions

  1. Enter your age, sex, height, weight, and activity level.
  2. Click Calculate plan to generate your estimated calorie level and food-group targets.
  3. Review the daily goals and compare them with your 3-day average intake from Cronometer.
  4. Click Print / Save PDF after your results appear.
  5. Upload that PDF with your completed worksheet in Canvas.
This classroom tool is designed to support dietary analysis assignments. It is not individualized medical nutrition therapy.
